What is WHAT NEXT?

WHAT NEXT? is an action-planning service designed to help people understand the practical steps they can take after deciding what they want to achieve.

Instead of leaving you wondering what to do next, WHAT NEXT? provides structured, easy-to-follow action plans.

Our Services

🏠 Housing

Practical steps for planning your housing search, checking properties and avoiding common housing mistakes.

💼 Jobs

Guidance for choosing a target role, preparing your CV, applying for jobs and preparing for interviews.

🎓 School

Steps for choosing a programme, checking requirements, preparing documents and completing applications.

🛂 Passport

General guidance for preparing documents and following the appropriate official passport application process.

🏢 Business

Practical steps for developing a business idea, understanding customers, planning costs and starting small.
