ABOUT WHAT NEXT?

Helping You Know What To Do Next

WHAT NEXT? is an action-planning service designed to help people turn their goals into practical next steps.

Whether you are looking for housing, a job, a school, a passport or a way to start a business, WHAT NEXT? helps you understand where to begin and what to do next.

Our Services

🏠 Housing

Practical guidance for planning a housing search, comparing options, verifying properties and avoiding common housing mistakes.

💼 Jobs

Action steps for choosing a target role, preparing your CV, finding genuine opportunities and preparing for applications and interviews.

🎓 School

Guidance for choosing a programme, comparing schools, checking requirements, preparing documents and following the application process.

🛂 Passport

General action-planning guidance for preparing documents and following the appropriate official passport process.

🏢 Business

Practical steps for developing a business idea, understanding customers, estimating costs and starting and improving a small business.
